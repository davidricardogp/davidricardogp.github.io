Scaleup Financing: How Can Policymakers Better Address the Growth Capital Gap for EU Deep-Tech Scaleups?

Josemaria Siota, Roger Singleton, and David Ricardo Gonzales Peña

Published June 17, 2026. IESE Business School, Scaleup Series, Policy Report 2 out of 3. Report number ACADEM-79055.

Abstract

This report examines how policymakers can better address the growth capital gap faced by EU deep-tech scaleups. It identifies core challenges, benchmarks policy initiatives, and explores ways to attract greater participation from private and corporate venture capital. The analysis draws on a literature review, workshops, and surveys involving 59 experts.
